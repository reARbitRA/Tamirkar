# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

Contributors and maintainers must provide a harassment-free, professional environment. Harassment, discriminatory language, threats, doxxing, and sharing private customer, technician, payment, or KYC information are prohibited.

Report conduct concerns privately to **conduct@oosta.app** or, until that mailbox is configured, to the repository owner through GitHub. Maintainers may remove content, restrict participation, or take other appropriate action. Retaliation against a reporter is not acceptable.
