# کارگاهِ استاد کاوه — Oosta lore

> **دور نینداز، درستش کن.**

In a Tehran where buying is too expensive and throwing away is treated as normal, a narrow door beneath the iron bazaar opens into a workshop that is larger on the inside.

Its keeper is **استاد کاوه**: the last apprentice of Kaveh the Blacksmith. His silver hair is held in three directions by static, a bench grinder, and a ceiling fan. A jeweller's loupe covers one eye, two other pairs of glasses are misplaced in plain sight, and his oxblood apron bears the permanent geography of solder burns and tea stains.

He does not make machines speak. He listens to them. He puts a brass stethoscope on a refrigerator compressor and says: **«این صداش کولر نیست، صدای یاتاقانه.»** He refuses to quote a price before opening the machine. On the wall, in hand-lettered paint, is his rule:

> **اول تشخیص، بعد هزینه.**

## The old flag, used again

Kaveh the Blacksmith raised his leather apron on a spear and made the **درفش کاویانی**. Oosta's apron shield is not nostalgia: it is a claim that the dignity of labour and the right to repair are connected.

The two serpents of Zahhāk have simply incorporated. They consume phones every eighteen months, fridges every six years, and any machine that fails just after its warranty. Their showroom spell is: **«صرف نمی‌کنه، بنداز دور، نو بخر.»**

Oosta's counter-spell is: **«دور نینداز، درستش کن.»**

## The workshop has rules of physics it does not explain

A locomotive boiler heats a samovar. Steam turns a washing-machine drum. The drum spins a Paykan alternator. A car radiator keeps solder paste cold in a refrigerator. The fridge's compressor is heard by a microphone built from a rotary telephone earpiece. That signal reaches a beige PC with a modern GPU tied inside it, then spills from a dot-matrix printer as a three-floor paper trail.

A fishing boat in the rafters is a paint booth. A taxi without wheels is reception. Fifty retired phones on a pegboard are microphones, cameras, and thermometers. A cat occupies a disassembled washing-machine drum. When the digital queue machine fails, Ostad repairs it with handwritten paper tickets and calls that a complete success.

## Relics that became product promises

- **گوشِ کاوه:** AI acoustic diagnosis must behave like a listening instrument. It can suggest, explain confidence, and send uncertainty to a human—it cannot pretend certainty.
- **شناسنامه:** each device gains a durable repair history, like a stamped Iranian booklet. A repair that cannot be traced cannot earn trust.
- **پولِ امانت:** the ۱۵٪ escrow is visible, held, and released only by a documented rule—not marketing copy.
- **کشوی سه‌طبقه:** اصلی · درجه‌یک · اقتصادی are clear choices with price and warranty in full view.
- **مُهر صنف:** verified technicians earn a unique guild punch and attach before/after proof.

This lore is a product standard. If a feature cannot make diagnosis clearer, price fairer, or a repair more accountable, it does not belong in Oosta.
