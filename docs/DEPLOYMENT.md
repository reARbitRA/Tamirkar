# Deployment & Distribution — Tamirkar (تعمیرکار)

## 🚀 Build Artifacts
- **Platform**: Android 7.0+ (API level 24 to 36).
- **Target App Stores**: Cafe Bazaar, Myket, Google Play Store.
- **Architecture**: Kotlin 2.2+, Jetpack Compose, Material 3, Room SQLite, Retrofit & OkHttp.
- **RTL & Persian Optimization**: Fully supported natively across all Jetpack Compose layouts.
