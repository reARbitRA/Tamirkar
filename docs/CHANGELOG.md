# Changelog — Tamirkar (تعمیرکار)

## [1.0.0-MVP] - 2026-08-19
### Initial Release
- Complete Android Super-App architecture built with Jetpack Compose & Material 3.
- Integrated Digital Passport (پاسپورت دیجیتال) for appliances and vehicles with health score modeling.
- 15% Escrow Warranty Protection (ضمانت اجرایی) with automated release lifecycle.
- 8 Autonomous Gemini AI Agents covering diagnosis, technician matching, arbitration, quality control, service reminders, anti-fraud, customer support, and marketing.
- Transparent Spare Parts Marketplace with Original/Grade A/Economy tiers.
- Technician Professional Mode with SOP checklist execution, before/after photo audits, earnings tracking, and academy.
- Full RTL Persian layout, currency formatters in Tomans (تومان), and responsive design.
